# 🔄 Week 3: End-to-End Data Analysis Process & Industry Use Cases

Notes and takeaways from Week 3 lectures covering the lifecycle of data problems, real-world constraints, and career specializations.

## 1. The 6-Step Data Analysis Lifecycle 🔁
An analyst follows a structured loop to transform a vague business worry into an evidence-backed decision:
1. **Ask:** Define the exact question and success metrics before touching data.
2. **Collect:** Gather relevant data from logs, surveys, or databases.
3. **Clean:** Handle typos, missing values, and standardize units (takes ~70-80% of the time).
4. **Analyse:** Segment data, spot patterns, and find root causes.
5. **Visualise:** Convert patterns into clutter-free, easy-to-read charts.
6. **Communicate:** Tell a clear story (Finding ➡️ Cause ➡️ Action Recommendation).

## 🍲 2. Practical Case Study: Canteen Food Wastage
- **The Problem:** Massive food wastage in a college canteen.
- **The Analysis (Exploratory):** Cleaning register entry formats and breaking down waste by dish type and calendar timeline.
- **The Insight:** Data isolated the waste heavily to **Rice**, specifically spiking on **Fridays and Exam Days** due to a drop in campus attendance while cooking volume remained unchanged.
- **The Actionable Recommendation:** Adjust cooking production metrics dynamically based on exam schedules and expected attendance.

## 🌐 3. The 5 Universal Jobs of Analytics (Across 10 Industries)
No matter the sector (E-commerce, Streaming, Fintech, Sports, or Healthcare), analytics always performs these 5 core roles:
- **Describe (What happened?):** Dashboards showing current revenue, metrics, or delivery trends.
- **Diagnose (Why did it happen?):** Drilling down to see why an application flow crashed or why user retention dropped.
- **Predict (What is likely to happen?):** Forecasting food demand volume at 8 PM or predicting Diwali order numbers.
- **Recommend (What should we do?):** Proposing dynamic price changes during live surge rush.
- **Personalise (What is right for this specific user?):** Driving algorithms like Netflix thumbnails, Spotify Wrapped, or Instagram Reels.

## 💼 4. Career Domains & Skills Matrix
- **Data Analyst:** Focuses on looking back at historical data to answer what/why via Excel, SQL, and BI dashboards.
- **Data Scientist:** Focuses on looking forward by building predictive models via Python and Machine Learning.
- **Specializations:** Same foundational analytical thinking applies whether specializing in Product, Marketing, Finance, or Operations.
