# 📊 Week 2: Data Fundamentals & Grammar of Data

Notes and takeaways from Week 2 lectures of the Data Analytics program at PW Skills.

## 1. The Number Trap 🪤
- Just because a value is a number does not mean it is quantitative (numerical).
- **Examples:** Jersey Numbers (Kohli's #18), Pin Codes, Phone Numbers, Aadhaar Numbers.
- **Analytical Rule:** These are actually **Qualitative (Nominal) Data**. Taking an average or adding them is mathematically meaningless. They serve only as identifiers.

## 2. Grammar of Data Categories
- **Qualitative (Categorical):**
  - **Nominal Scale:** Pure labels with no natural order (e.g., Payment methods like UPI, Card, Cash).
  - **Ordinal Scale:** Ordered labels with unequal, unmeasurable gaps (e.g., App ratings 1⭐️ to 5⭐️, Customer Satisfaction).
- **Quantitative (Numerical):**
  - **Discrete:** Countable whole numbers (e.g., Wickets in a match, App downloads).
  - **Continuous:** Measurable values where decimals are valid (e.g., Food delivery time, Rainfall).

## 3. Data Sensitivity & Ethics 🛡️
- **PII (Personally Identifiable Information):** Data that identifies a specific person (e.g., Name, Phone number, Aadhaar).
- **Sensitive Data:** A deeper layer carrying higher risk if leaked (e.g., Financial transactions, Health records).
- **Regulation:** Underlined the importance of user privacy and compliance with India's **DPDP Act 2023**.
